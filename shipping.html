Shipping & Returns

Pay by 12pm Melbourne time on a business day and your order leaves that day. Business days are Monday to Friday, except Victorian public holidays.

Delivery

Standard
Arrives 2 to 7 business days after dispatch. Priced by weight and destination at checkout.
Express
Arrives 1 to 3 business days after dispatch. Priced by weight and destination at checkout.
Rods and combos
Rods and rod and reel combos go by courier at a flat $29.95 anywhere in Australia.
Dangerous goods
Lithium batteries, pressurised cans and some marine fluids go by Standard only, by road only, and not to PO boxes. Allow 1 to 3 extra business days.

Pick up instead

Choose Click & Collect at checkout and pick your order up from our shop at 105 Shannon Ave, Manifold Heights. It's free, usually ready within an hour during store hours, and held for 7 days from the ready email. How Click & Collect works

Returns

Changed your mind? You have 30 days to return new, unused and unopened items in their original packaging. You pay the return postage, and the original shipping isn't refunded.

To start a return, email sales@alloutfishing.com.au with your order number. We usually inspect returns within 2 business days of receiving them.

We can't take these back for a change of mind:

  • bait, berley and consumables
  • spooled or cut line
  • opened soft plastics
  • licences and gift cards
  • worn apparel where hygiene matters
  • custom rods, reels with custom line and special orders

Damaged or wrong items

Faulty, damaged or not-as-described items are covered by Australian Consumer Law, and we pay the return postage when the problem is on us. Email sales@alloutfishing.com.au with your order number.

Questions about an order?

Call us on 03 5222 3257 or send us a message.
