About Us

We're fishermen, and we sell fishing tackle as well. We use this gear, and we fish the same waters you fish.

Our story

All Out Fishing is a family-run tackle shop at 105 Shannon Ave in Manifold Heights, Geelong. Peri runs it with Michael, Peri's dad, and the crew below.

Because we've done it ourselves, we can give you advice from experience. Ask us how to work a lure so it swims properly, or what changes when there's a crosswind. We do what you do.
