What we need from you

A few things only you can supply.

  1. 1. Crew photos and a crew note

    Photos of the crew, and a short crew note for the product page.

  2. 2. A highlight reel

    A short reel of the crew on the water for the home page.

  3. 3. Permission for the fishing finder

    This one is only for when the finder is built for real. The Victorian Fisheries Authority's stocking data is licensed for non-commercial use, so a live version needs their written permission, or the data under CC BY. Their email is victorianfisheries@vfa.vic.gov.au.

    Things worth tidying in Retail Express or your shop system. You'll know best which of these are right as they are.
